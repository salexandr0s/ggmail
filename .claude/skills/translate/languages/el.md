# Greek (`el`)

Greek follows Apple's conventions in macOS Mail and AppKit for address, label form, capitalization, punctuation and the ellipsis. Its words follow two rules:

- **An OS label takes its platform's word.** An entry whose `msgctxt` is `macOS`, `Windows` or `Linux` is a label the operating system names its own way, such as the File menu or Paste. A label only macOS shows, such as the Window menu or Hide Others, is an OS label too and carries `macOS` alone. `macOS` takes Apple's word (Apple Mail's for a mail menu item), `Windows` Microsoft's and `Linux` GNOME's, from [Platform words](#platform-words) below. Only the word changes: address, capitalization, punctuation and the ellipsis stay this sheet's on every platform.
- **Everything else takes the house word**, from this sheet and the glossary, by the [source order](../source-order.md): Gmail's word for a term Gmail also shows, Google's for Google's names, and in ggmail's own copy Chrome's word where Chrome has the term, else the apps' word the glossary counts and marks agent-chosen, with its tally. Apple is never the authority for a house word; its macOS apps are one vote of seven when the glossary counts the apps' word. ggmail's own copy reads the same on every platform, and each rule in `el.json` gives its word's source.

Where this sheet and a habit from other software disagree, this sheet wins.

## Address

The formal plural, _εσείς_ and _σας_, with the verb in the second person plural, as Apple and Chrome write: _σας_ is in 5452 of Apple's messages and 2041 of Chrome's, _εσύ_, _εσένα_ or _σου_ in 65 and 29. The pronoun stays lowercase, and the verb carries the person, so _εσείς_ is mostly left out: _Θέλετε σίγουρα …;_, _τους λογαριασμούς σας_. Never _εσύ_, _εσένα_ or _σου_ for the user, and never a singular imperative (_Επίλεξε_, _Άνοιξε_). One kind of string is the user's own words: a prompt the app sends to the mail assistant for them addresses the assistant in the singular, _Βρες μηνύματα σχετικά με_.

## Buttons and menu items

The action noun: _Απάντηση_, _Διαγραφή_, _Αποθήκευση_, _Ακύρωση_, _Κλείσιμο καρτέλας_. Never an imperative (_Διαγράψτε_, _Κλείστε_) or a verb in the first person (_Διαγράφω_).

## Label forms

The form each kind of label takes, decided before the language's first run from Apple's and Chrome's Greek. Every label of a kind takes its form, whatever its English does. Greek names a command by its action noun, never by a verb: the Chrome labels that are exactly Cancel, Close, Delete, Remove, Add, Edit, Open, Show, Hide and Save are _Ακύρωση_ (Chrome 4/4), _Κλείσιμο_ (4/4), _Διαγραφή_ (6/6), _Κατάργηση_ (4/4), _Προσθήκη_ (2/2), _Επεξεργασία_ (5/5), _Άνοιγμα_ (3/3), _Εμφάνιση_ (1/1), _Απόκρυψη_ (2/2) and _Αποθήκευση_ (2/2), and AppKit's menus read _Αντιγραφή_, _Επικόλληση_ and _Απόκρυψη άλλων_. A sentence whose English opens with a verb splits by who acts. Counted over the messages whose English is one sentence of four or more words opening with that verb, what the software does is an action noun: Apple opens 21 of 21 Show… sentences with _Εμφάνιση_ and 75 of 76 Automatically… sentences with a form of _αυτόματος_, and Chrome 2 of 2 Show… with _Εμφάνιση_ and 5 of 6 Send… with _Αποστολή_. What the user is asked to do is the formal imperative: Apple opens 222 of 223 Choose… and 101 of 102 Select… sentences with _Επιλέξτε_, 73 of 93 Add… with _Προσθέστε_ and 84 of 109 Set… with _Ορίστε_, _Καθορίστε_ or _Ρυθμίστε_, and Chrome 4 of 4 Choose… and 6 of 7 Select… with _Επιλέξτε_.

- **Button:** The action noun, in sentence style, as Chrome writes its buttons: _Ακύρωση_, _Αποθήκευση_, _Κλείσιμο καρτέλας_, _Επανεκκίνηση τώρα_; never an imperative (_Αποθηκεύστε_). Two set phrases have no action noun and keep Chrome's formal imperative: Try again _Δοκιμάστε ξανά_ (Chrome 4/6) and Learn more _Μάθετε περισσότερα_ (Chrome 7/7). A question in a dialog's title or button takes the action noun and the Greek question mark, as Chrome writes 29 of its 42 Delete…? and Remove…? questions: _Επαναφορά του ggmail στην αρχική κατάσταση;_
- **Menu bar item:** The action noun, in sentence style, the app menu the titlebar button pops up on Windows and Linux included: _Κλείσιμο καρτέλας_, _Επιλογή επόμενης καρτέλας_, _Διαχείριση λογαριασμών_; an item that names a menu or a place is its noun: _Προβολή_, _Λήψεις_.
- **Context or popup menu item:** The action noun, in sentence style, as Chrome writes its own, _Άνοιγμα συνδέσμου σε νέα καρτέλα_ (Chrome 3/3): _Αντιγραφή συνδέσμου_, _Κλείσιμο άλλων καρτελών_, _Άνοιγμα στο προεπιλεγμένο πρόγραμμα περιήγησης_.
- **Select option:** An option that names an action takes the action noun; any other is the word itself, as Chrome writes it: an adverb (_Αυτόματα_, _Πάντα_, _Ποτέ_), a noun (_Προεπιλογή_) or an adjective agreeing with the setting's noun in gender and number, in the nominative, as _Σκούρο_ under _Θέμα_ and _Στενό_ under _Πλάτος_. None agrees with what it stands for: _Κανένα_ for a color, _Καμία_ for an app. A setting's label ends in a preposition only where its options read the same in the case the preposition governs: _Ειδοποιήσεις για_, whose neuter options are the same in the nominative and the accusative.
- **Setting label never ends with:** a preposition, unless its options read the same in the case it governs: _από_ _για_ _με_ _σε_ _προς_ _κατά_ _μετά_ _παρά_ _αντί_ _μέχρι_ _έως_ _ως_ _χωρίς_ _δίχως_ _πριν_ _μεταξύ_ _εκτός_ _εντός_ _λόγω_ _μέσω_ _ανά_ _επί_ _περί_ _υπό_. Allowed: Notify for _για_, whose options, _Όλα τα νέα μηνύματα_ and _Μόνο σημαντικά_, are neuter and read the same in the accusative it governs; Hibernate after _από_, whose options, _1 λεπτό_, _30 λεπτά_ and _1 ώρα_, read the same in the accusative; Sort by _κατά_, Chrome's own label, _Ταξινόμηση κατά_ (Chrome 1/1), whose options read the same in the accusative; To _Προς_, Gmail's compose label for the recipients of a suggested email, which stands before email addresses that take no case.
- **Checkbox or switch label:** The action noun, in sentence style, as Chrome writes its own, _Εμφάνιση γραμμής σελιδοδεικτών_ (Chrome 4/4) and _Αποκλεισμός cookie τρίτων_ (Chrome 1/1): _Εμφάνιση σήματος μη αναγνωσμένων_, _Απόκρυψη λογοτύπου Gmail_, _Άνοιγμα φακέλου μετά την ολοκλήρωση_. A label that names a feature rather than an action is its noun: _Επιτάχυνση υλικού_. A standing rule, which the English opens with Always, Never or Don't, may take Chrome's _να_ clause instead, as 53 of its 67 Always… labels, 14 of its 17 Never… and all 44 of its Don't allow… do: _Να μην εμφανιστεί ξανά_; the action noun with _πάντα_ serves as well where it reads cleanly, as Chrome's _Άνοιγμα πάντα με πρόγραμμα προβολής συστήματος_ (Chrome 2/2): _Σύνταξη νέων μηνυμάτων πάντα σε νέο παράθυρο_.
- **Checkbox that picks what an action applies to:** A noun phrase naming what it picks, in sentence style, never the action noun, as Chrome's Cached images and files is in its Delete browsing data dialog (Chrome 1/1): _Εικόνες και αρχεία στην κρυφή μνήμη_, _Cookie και άλλα δεδομένα ιστοτόπων_.
- **Setting description:** The action noun, as its label has, ending in a period, for a description that says what the setting does: _Εμφάνιση ειδοποίησης όταν ολοκληρωθεί μια λήψη._, _Απόκρυψη του λογοτύπου Gmail στην επάνω αριστερή γωνία._ One that tells the user what to pick or supply, which the English opens with Add, Choose, Select or Set, takes the formal imperative: _Επιλέξτε τον ήχο ειδοποιήσεων._, _Ορίστε την ένταση των ήχων ειδοποιήσεων._ One that says what the setting holds is a noun phrase, _Τοποθεσία αποθήκευσης ληφθέντων αρχείων._, and an instruction for a step the user takes, such as Swipe…, the formal imperative: _Σύρετε δύο δάχτυλα αριστερά ή δεξιά …_
- **Setting description never opens with:** the imperative of the English verb a description opens with, in its first word or the first word after a leading phrase's comma, formal for a verb that says what the setting does and singular for Add, Choose, Select, Set and Swipe, whose descriptions take the formal imperative: Add _Πρόσθεσε_; Ask _Ρωτήστε_ _Ζητήστε_; Block _Αποκλείστε_ _Μπλοκάρετε_; Check _Ελέγξτε_; Choose _Επίλεξε_ _Διάλεξε_; Close _Κλείστε_; Detect _Εντοπίστε_ _Ανιχνεύστε_; Display _Εμφανίστε_ _Προβάλετε_; Export _Εξαγάγετε_; Extend _Επεκτείνετε_ _Εφαρμόστε_; Hide _Αποκρύψτε_ _Κρύψτε_; List _Παραθέστε_ _Εμφανίστε_; Mark _Επισημάνετε_ _Σημειώστε_ _Σημάνετε_; Move _Μετακινήστε_ _Μεταφέρετε_; Open _Ανοίξτε_; Play _Αναπαραγάγετε_ _Παίξτε_; Prompt _Ζητήστε_ _Ρωτήστε_; Receive _Λάβετε_; Recolor _Αλλάξτε_ _Χρωματίστε_; Remember _Απομνημονεύστε_ _Θυμηθείτε_ _Διατηρήστε_; Render _Αποδώστε_; Run _Εκτελέστε_; Select _Επίλεξε_; Send _Στείλτε_ _Αποστείλετε_; Set _Όρισε_ _Καθόρισε_ _Ρύθμισε_; Show _Εμφανίστε_ _Δείξτε_ _Δείτε_; Signs _Αποσυνδεθείτε_; Start _Ξεκινήστε_ _Εκκινήστε_; Stop _Αποτρέψτε_ _Σταματήστε_ _Διακόψτε_; Swipe _Σύρε_; Unload _Εκφορτώστε_ _Αποφορτώστε_; Use _Χρησιμοποιήστε_. `scripts/translate-rules.test.ts` fails on one, except a description that is itself an instruction the test names, and fails when a description opens with an English verb this line lacks.
- **OS label:** Its platform's word from [Platform words](#platform-words), in the form its source writes it, the action noun or a noun for all three: _Επικόλληση_, _Τερματισμός {appName}_ on macOS and _Έξοδος από το {appName}_ on Windows, _Αρχείο_.

## Capitalization

Sentence style: only the first word and proper nouns take a capital, in labels and titles alike (_Κλείσιμο άλλων καρτελών_, never _Κλείσιμο Άλλων Καρτελών_). A word set whole in capitals carries no tonos: _ΡΥΘΜΙΣΕΙΣ_. A label that opens something further ends in an ellipsis straight after the word, with no space, as Apple writes 3139 of its 3197 labels that end in one: _Ρυθμίσεις…_, _Κατάργηση λογαριασμού…_.

## Settings

_Ρυθμίσεις_, Chrome's word (Chrome 432/432). The app-menu item with `msgctxt` `macOS` is _Ρυθμίσεις…_, as AppKit writes it; with `Windows` or `Linux` it is _Ρυθμίσεις…_ too, Microsoft's and GNOME's word with the English's ellipsis.

## Access keys

An `&` in the English marks the access key on Windows and Linux. Keep one `&` inline, before a letter of the Greek word. The English letter is a Latin one no Greek word holds, so the key is a Greek letter of the label's own word that no other label in the same menu uses. macOS drops the `&`. `packages/app/access-keys.test.ts` holds every catalog to this: it builds each menu and context menu Windows and Linux show, and fails when two items in one menu share a letter.

The page and text-field context menu, by that policy, on Windows: `&Learn Spelling` _&Εκμάθηση ορθογραφίας_, the one item Chrome has no message for under its English, `&Search with Google` _Ανα&ζήτηση με την Google_, Chrome's words (Chrome 1/1), `Cu&t` _Απο&κοπή_ and `&Copy` _&Αντιγραφή_, Chrome's words and keys (Chrome 2/2 each), `&Paste` _Ε&πικόλληση_, Chrome's word with the key on π, since a misspelled word shows it beside _&Εκμάθηση ορθογραφίας_, `Paste and &Match Style` _Επικόλληση και αντιστοίχιση &μορφής_, `Select &All` _Επιλογή ό&λων_, Chrome's words with the key on λ, since an image shows it beside _Αποθήκευση εικόνας &ως…_, `Sa&ve Image As…` _Αποθήκευση εικόνας &ως…_, Chrome's words (Chrome 2/2) with one of its two keys, `Copy Link Addr&ess` _Αντιγραφή διεύ&θυνσης συνδέσμου_, Chrome's words (Chrome 3/3) with the key on θ, which no item beside it takes, `Cop&y Image` _Αντιγραφή &εικόνας_, Chrome's words (Chrome 2/2), `C&opy Image Address` _Αντιγραφή της &διεύθυνσης εικόνας_, Chrome's words (Chrome 2/2). On Linux, where its words differ: `Paste and &Match Style` _Επικόλληση και αντιστοίχιση &στιλ_.

## Plural categories

`one` and `other`. Every plural carries exactly these two, `=0` and other exact matches aside: `{count, plural, one {# καρτέλα} other {# καρτέλες}}`. The noun after `#` stands in the case its sentence gives it: _# αρχείο_ and _# αρχεία_, but _Αποτυχία ενημέρωσης # επέκτασης_ and _# επεκτάσεων_.

## Terms that stay English

Gmail, Google Workspace, ggmail, in Latin letters, never transliterated and never inflected. The article carries the case and the gender: neuter for an app or a service, _το Gmail_, _στο ggmail_, _του Gmail_, as Chrome writes Gmail in 12 of the 15 messages that name it and never with the feminine; feminine for Google the company, _με την Google_, as Chrome writes it in 74 messages against 6 with a neuter article.

## Traps

- **Placeholders and articles.** An article or adjective before a placeholder must agree in gender, number and case with every value it can take, which it usually cannot. Put a noun that carries them before the placeholder: _Η επέκταση {name}_, _Ο λογαριασμός {label}_, _στην καρτέλα «{title}»_, never _Το {name}_.
- **Gendered forms.** A participle or adjective about the user shows gender. The formal plural takes the masculine plural, as Chrome writes it (_Είστε συνδεδεμένοι_, in 30 of Chrome's messages with signed in, none of them singular), or the sentence turns to a verb: _Έχετε συνδεθεί_.
- **The final sigma.** A word ends in _ς_ and has _σ_ everywhere else: _καρτέλες_, _Ρυθμίσεις_. A word cut or built by hand, such as a stem before a placeholder, is where the wrong one slips in.
- **The article's final ν.** The masculine _τον_, _στον_, _έναν_ and _αυτόν_ always keep it, as Chrome writes _στον φάκελο_ in 30 messages against 4 with _στο φάκελο_ and _τον λογαριασμό_ in 102 against 1. The feminine _την_ and _στην_ keep it before a vowel and before κ, π, τ, ξ, ψ, μπ, ντ, γκ, τσ and τζ (_στην καρτέλα_, _την επέκταση_) and drop it elsewhere (_στη γραμμή_, _τη λίστα_), as Chrome does.
- **Punctuation.** Quotation marks are «…» (U+00AB, U+00BB), as Apple writes them in 2096 of its 2143 messages whose English has a curly quote; Chrome's straight quotes are not the model. A question ends in the Greek question mark, the semicolon _;_ (U+003B), never the Latin _?_ and never U+037E, which looks the same and is another character to a search: _Διαγραφή όλων των δεδομένων;_ An ellipsis is the one character _…_, never three full stops.
- **Numbers and units.** A plain space joins a number to its unit, as Chrome writes it (Chrome 57 of 59 such messages): _5 λεπτά_, _30 ημέρες_, and in a plural _# ημέρες_.
- **Email.** Email as a medium is _ηλεκτρονικό ταχυδρομείο_ (Chrome 57/57), an email address a _διεύθυνση ηλεκτρονικού ταχυδρομείου_ (Chrome 17/18) and one email a _μήνυμα_, Gmail's word. Never the Latin _email_ or _e-mail_, which Chrome writes in none of its messages.
- **House words, as Chrome writes them.** A notification is an _ειδοποίηση_, never Apple's _γνωστοποίηση_, which belongs only in a `macOS` label. A link is a _σύνδεσμος_, a folder a _φάκελος_, never _κατάλογος_, an update an _ενημέρωση_, an upgrade an _αναβάθμιση_, and restarting is _επανεκκίνηση_. The titlebar is the _γραμμή τίτλου_, the tab strip the _γραμμή καρτελών_, the sidebar the _πλευρική γραμμή_, and a button on screen a _κουμπί_; a _πλήκτρο_ is a key on the keyboard. A password is a _κωδικός πρόσβασης_ in full, a passkey a _κλειδί πρόσβασης_, the clipboard the _πρόχειρο_, an extension an _επέκταση_ and a bookmark a _σελιδοδείκτης_. Pinning a tab is _καρφίτσωμα_ and unpinning it _ξεκαρφίτσωμα_. The launcher is the _λειτουργία εκκίνησης_. A hibernated tab is _ανενεργή_, Chrome's word for the tabs its Memory Saver unloads, never _αδρανοποίηση_, the operating system's sleep. Hibernating has no action noun of its own, since _απενεργοποίηση_ reads as disabling the tab, so a label for it names the state instead: _Ανενεργή μετά από_, _Ανενεργή όταν δεν χρησιμοποιείται_, _Καρτέλες που γίνονται ανενεργές_. Style is _στιλ_ (Chrome 28 messages, Apple 395), never _στυλ_. Auto is the adverb _Αυτόματα_ in every setting. The sound Linen is _Λινό_.
- **macOS's own elements.** The menu bar, the Dock, Finder and System Settings take Apple's names in ggmail's own copy too, since they name Apple's interface and show only on macOS: _γραμμή μενού_ (ControlCenterSettings.appex `Localizable` `Show in Menu Bar`), _Dock_ (DesktopSettings.appex `Localizable` `Dock`), _Finder_ and _Ρυθμίσεις συστήματος_ (System Settings `InfoPlist` `CFBundleDisplayName`). Do Not Disturb is Apple's _Μην ενοχλείτε_, quoted «…» inside a label or a sentence.
- **The file manager.** It shows only on Linux, so it takes GNOME's word, _διαχειριστής αρχείων_ (nautilus `_Open in File Manager`), never _διαχείριση αρχείων_.

## Error messages

Say what happened, without _Λυπούμαστε_, _Συγγνώμη_ or _Δυστυχώς_: _Δεν ήταν δυνατή η προσθήκη του λογαριασμού._, as Apple writes _Δεν ήταν δυνατό να μετονομαστεί η θυρίδα «%1$@»._

## Common labels

House words for everyday actions, as Chrome writes its labels, in this sheet's label form. A string that names something Gmail also shows, such as the Undo button in the message-sent toast, takes Gmail's word from the glossary instead, and an entry with a platform `msgctxt` takes [its platform's word](#platform-words). Delete and Remove are two words, as Chrome writes them: Remove account never reads as deleting it. The labels:

- Cancel _Ακύρωση_, OK _OK_ in Latin letters, Undo _Αναίρεση_, Close _Κλείσιμο_, Delete _Διαγραφή_, Remove _Κατάργηση_, Add _Προσθήκη_, Save _Αποθήκευση_, Edit _Επεξεργασία_, Open _Άνοιγμα_, Show _Εμφάνιση_, Hide _Απόκρυψη_, Quit _Έξοδος_, Reload _Επανάληψη φόρτωσης_, Try Again _Δοκιμάστε ξανά_, Learn More… _Μάθετε περισσότερα…_
- Close Tab _Κλείσιμο καρτέλας_, Close Other Tabs _Κλείσιμο άλλων καρτελών_, Pin Tab _Καρφίτσωμα καρτέλας_, Copy Link _Αντιγραφή συνδέσμου_, Mark as Read _Σημείωση ως αναγνωσμένο_, Gmail's word, Add Account… _Προσθήκη λογαριασμού…_, Inspect Element _Έλεγχος_, as Chrome's Inspect in its page context menu
- _Θέλετε σίγουρα να διαγράψετε αυτό το μήνυμα;_ (Are you sure you want to delete this message?), as Apple opens 325 of its 334 such questions
- _Ο διαχειριστής σας έχει απενεργοποιήσει αυτήν τη λειτουργία._
